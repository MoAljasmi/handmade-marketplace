About us

Hirfa means “craft”

Hirfa brings handmade products from small makers in the UAE to customers who value quality, local heritage and pieces that nobody else has. We handle the website, orders and delivery, so makers can spend their time making.

Browse and search

Receive and review

Your order arrives in 2 to 4 working days. Tell other customers what you think.

Delivery

  • We deliver to all seven emirates in 2 to 4 working days.
  • Delivery costs AED 15 and is free for orders from AED 200.
  • You can cancel an order from My account while it is still pending.

Payment and prices

  • All prices are in UAE dirhams (AED) and include 5% VAT.
  • Pay by cash or card when the courier arrives.
  • This website never asks for or stores card numbers.

Privacy

  • We collect your name, e-mail, phone number and delivery address only to process your orders.
  • Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored in our database.
  • Each customer can see only their own profile and orders. Reviews show your first name and last initial.
  • You can update your details at any time, or ask us to delete your account.

Reviews you can trust

  • Only customers who ordered a product can review it.
  • Each customer can leave one review per product, and can edit or delete it.
  • We do not pay for reviews or remove honest negative ones.
